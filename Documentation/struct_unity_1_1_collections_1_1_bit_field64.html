ulong Unity.Collections.BitField64.GetBits ( int pos,
int numBits = 1 )

Returns one or more contiguous bits from the bit field as the lower bits of a ulong.

Paramètres
posPosition in the bit field of the first bit to get (must be 0-63).
numBitsNumber of bits to get (must be 1-64).
Exceptions
ArgumentExceptionThrown if pos or numBits are out of bounds or if pos + numBits exceeds 64.
Renvoie
The requested range of bits from the bit field stored in the least-significant bits of a ulong. All other bits of the ulong will be 0.
