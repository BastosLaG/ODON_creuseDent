Référence de la classe UnityEngine.XR.Interaction.Toolkit.Samples.VisionOS.VisionOSFarCaster

The visionOS Far Caster serves as a means routing the spatial touch interaction collider to the Near-Far Interactor. This class was mainly tested for Bounded and Unbounded mixed reality modes. Description détaillée

The visionOS Far Caster serves as a means routing the spatial touch interaction collider to the Near-Far Interactor. This class was mainly tested for Bounded and Unbounded mixed reality modes. It may also work in VR mode, but some additional testing and iteration may be required.
